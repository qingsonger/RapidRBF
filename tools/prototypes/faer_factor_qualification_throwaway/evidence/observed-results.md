## What remains possible

- Public L/B/permutation and full-pivot L/U/P/Q data are sufficient to define RapidRBF-owned logical records; raw faer allocations are not a format.
- Low-level calls can accept explicit `Par` and `MemStack`; an outer controller can add permits, process isolation, and metadata controls.
- A future probe may qualify an instrumented faer fork or an isolated adapter with an accepted quota allocator and hard-cancellation design.

Those are follow-up hypotheses, not evidence that this candidate passed or that every possible RapidRBF-owned faer adapter fails.
