# Separate measurement evidence from acceptance judgment

RapidRBF will collect Polatory/RapidRBF comparisons through immutable paired measurement plans with declared lifecycle profiles, a common process-isolated subject boundary, preregistered pair order, required channels, and append-only attempts. Retries append rather than replace evidence and stay outside primary summaries unless a separately governed policy admits them; canonical optimized measurements remain separate from linked instrumented or RapidRBF-only phase diagnostics, whose timing cannot replace the canonical pair, and an optional diagnostic may be unavailable only when every comparison-required channel is present. Numerical and resource threshold sets remain separately versioned inputs to judgment, so complete audited evidence without the relevant sets is `COLLECTED, UNJUDGED`; the additional retention and separation are accepted to prevent selective omission, contaminated performance claims, and threshold or backend choices leaking into collection. The authoritative decision and throwaway prototype are recorded in [Prototype the differential and resource measurement harness](https://github.com/qingsonger/RapidRBF/issues/15).
