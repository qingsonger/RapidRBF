# Judge factor solves against the frozen system

RapidRBF judges factor-solve forward health against a candidate-independent certified enclosure of the exact solution of the frozen binary64 `(A,b)` system, with the manufactured declared vector retained only to construct `b` and for diagnostics. Oracle uncertainty is enclosed before candidate execution and yields `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `INDETERMINATE` under the unchanged threshold; the extra reference-certification cost is accepted because rounding `A*x` can make the declared vector an unsound oracle, while candidate agreement and backward error cannot replace forward-solution authority. The accepted prototype and complete requalification plan are recorded in [Repair the projected-factor solution-health authority and freeze a replacement qualification plan](https://github.com/qingsonger/RapidRBF/issues/46).
